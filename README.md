# Zero Hunger (Smart Food Cycle)

A food donation platform that connects people and organisations with surplus food to NGOs and those in need.
Developed for **Innovation and Design Thinking – II** (Semester IV, B.Tech CSE) at Silver Oak University, Ahmedabad.

## The Problem
Large amounts of edible food are wasted at weddings, events, restaurants and canteens, while many people go hungry.
There is no simple, direct link between those who have surplus food and those who need it.

## Our Solution
Zero Hunger is a platform where donors post surplus food and NGOs or volunteers collect it before it is wasted.
It supports UN Sustainable Development Goal 2: Zero Hunger.

## User Roles
- **Donor:** restaurants, institutes, event halls or individuals who post food details (type, quantity, expiry, pickup location)
- **Receiver:** NGOs or individuals who browse and request available food
- **Admin:** approves or blocks users, monitors donations and handles reports and feedback
- **Volunteer:** helps with pickup and delivery

## Key Features
- Donor and receiver registration and login
- Donation form with food type, quantity, best-before date and pickup location
- Donation stats dashboard
- Search and matching of nearby receivers (planned)
- Pickup scheduling and notifications (planned)
- Food safety checklist and user verification (planned)
- Rewards, badges and leaderboard for active donors (planned)
